# Refund Policy

Customers may receive a refund when an order is cancelled before shipment.

For delayed shipments, a refund may be requested if the package has not
been delivered within 7 days after the estimated delivery date.

A shipment that is still within its estimated delivery window does not
qualify for a delay-based refund.

Refunds for damaged or incorrect items are handled separately after the
item has been delivered and reviewed.
